# LUNA 2.0 Business Model

## Positioning

LUNA is an investment decision platform, not a prediction subscription.

Customer value is access to a repeatable workflow:

DATA → EVIDENCE → DECISION → RISK → ACTION

## Customer segments

### Investor
Portfolio and fundamental/market decision support.

### Pro Trader / Advanced Investor
Real-time workflow, risk controls, paper trading and advanced research.

### Quant
Strategy creation and validation.

### Enterprise
CIO, portfolio, research, risk, governance and API infrastructure.

## Packaging

| Tier | Purpose | Core value |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Acquisition | Market/basic discovery |
| Investor | Monetize retail | Portfolio + research + decision |
| Pro | Power users | Advanced risk + paper + alerts |
| Quant | Research users | Formula Lab + validation |
| Enterprise | B2B | Multi-user intelligence infrastructure |

Initial prices are hypotheses only and must be validated with customers.

## Revenue streams

1. Subscription
2. Usage: compute/API/data/export
3. Enterprise contracts
4. Future ecosystem revenue: strategy/research licensing and marketplace

## Unit economics

Track:

MRR, ARR, ARPU, CAC, LTV, churn, free-to-paid conversion, expansion, data cost/user, AI cost/user, compute cost/user, cloud cost/user, gross margin and contribution margin.

Heavy compute must be entitlement/usage metered.

## North-star business metric

**Verified Investment Decisions Managed by LUNA**

This should be paired with commercial metrics rather than replacing them.

## Moat logic

The product becomes harder to replace as it accumulates:

customer history + strategy history + evidence + execution outcomes + portfolio context.
