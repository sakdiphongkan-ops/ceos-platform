# LUNA 2.0 Foundation

LUNA is the **Investment Intelligence Operating System**.

Core loop:

DATA → RESEARCH → ANALYSIS → DECISION → RISK → PORTFOLIO → EXECUTION → OUTCOME → LEARNING

## Product layers

- LUNA Market — market, watchlists, data and discovery.
- LUNA Invest — portfolio, valuation and fundamental intelligence.
- LUNA Quant — formula/factor research, backtest, OOS, holdout, walk-forward and paper trading.
- LUNA Execution — broker connectivity, orders, fills and execution analytics.
- LUNA Enterprise — CIO, research, risk, governance, reporting and APIs.

## CEOS relationship

CEOS becomes the **Fundamental Intelligence Engine** inside LUNA:

DCF, DDM, SOTP, multiples, scenario analysis, margin of safety, financial analysis, SEC/IR research and portfolio intelligence.

LUNA contributes market, quantitative, risk and execution intelligence.

The two systems meet at the **Decision Engine**.

## Core moat

1. Data lineage
2. Evidence/research history
3. Strategy Genome
4. Decision history
5. Execution intelligence
6. Personal investment memory
7. Ecosystem/network effects

## Non-goals

- Do not build a signal-only product.
- Do not claim benchmark results as user-formula results.
- Do not allow unverified real-money execution.
- Do not let agents overwrite evidence or historical research.
- Do not redesign approved UI without a product reason.
